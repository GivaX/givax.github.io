Featured research / 2026–present

Seeing what batteries hide.

At ASU, I study battery manufacturing defects in CT scans. I built rotation alignment and multi-slice preprocessing, benchmarked ResNet and DINO vision embeddings, and found that 30-40% of batteries per batch was enough to capture the anomalous batch in subsampling experiments.

Current research / Robotics

Teaching an arm to act.

Under Dr. Ransalu Senanayake at ASU, I am training and evaluating state-of-the-art robot policies on recorded demonstration datasets to control an SO-101 arm. I am exploring ACT, Diffusion Policy, SmolVLA, and GR00T for manipulation tasks including pick-and-place and peg pickup and insertion. This work is ongoing alongside my battery research.
